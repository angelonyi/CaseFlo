# CaseFlo — Business Brief

## The Business

CaseFlo is a client portal for small law firms in Nigeria. It allows clients to log in and check the progress of their legal matters, view approved documents, check their bills, pay legal fees online, and ask questions about their cases.

## The Users

CaseFlo is designed for adults aged 18–65 who are clients of small law firms. They may use Android phones, iPhones, tablets, or computers. Some have fast internet connections, while others rely on slower mobile data. Users have different levels of experience with technology, so the app must be easy to understand and use without special training.

## The One Thing That Must Work

Clients must be able to log in securely and see the correct information about their own legal matters. They must never see another client's private information.

## Three Questions Clients Ask Repeatedly

1. What is happening with my case?
2. How much do I owe the law firm?
3. When is my next appointment or court date?

CaseFlo should answer these questions using the law firm's records. If the information is unavailable, it should direct the client to the appropriate staff member instead of guessing.

## How the Interface Should Feel

The interface should feel professional, clear, and trustworthy. Legal matters can be stressful, so clients should be able to find important information quickly without struggling to understand the app. Text should be easy to read, buttons should be clearly labelled, and pages should work well on mobile phones and slower internet connections.
