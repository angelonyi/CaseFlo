# CaseFlo Shadows

## 1. Shadow Styles

CaseFlo uses three shadow styles: soft, medium, and hard. Each shadow uses the primary brand colour, `#0044E3`, to keep the shadows consistent with the design.

| Shadow Style | X Offset | Y Offset | Blur | Spread | Colour | Opacity |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|---:|
| Soft | 1.5px | 6px | 3px | 0px | `#0044E3` | 12% |
| Medium | 4px | 6px | 24px | 0px | `#0044E3` | 18% |
| Hard | 6px | 10px | 20px | 0px | `#0044E3` | 20% |

## 2. Why Use the Brand Colour?

Blue shadows give CaseFlo a subtle blue tint that connects the shadows to the brand's visual identity. Black shadows look more neutral, while blue shadows help the interface feel more connected to the brand.

## 3. Consistency

These three shadow styles will be reused across CaseFlo to maintain a consistent appearance throughout the interface.
